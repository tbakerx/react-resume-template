# Amos Khan Portfolio

The personal portfolio of Amos Khan, a PE teacher in training, showing the teaching apps Amos has built, plus research and coaching.

## Language

**Project**:
Any piece of work shown as a card in the Projects section: an app, a research output, or an activity such as photography or coaching.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, work

**Featured project**:
A Project that is a live app Amos built for teaching. Only Featured projects carry the Featured badge, and they are listed first.
_Avoid_: Highlight, flagship

**Co-creator**:
A person who built a Featured project together with Amos. A Co-creator is credited by first name only.
_Avoid_: Collaborator, partner
